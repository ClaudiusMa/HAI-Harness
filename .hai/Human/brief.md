# Product Brief — HAI-Harness

Updated: 2026-09-09

## Direction

Develop HAI-Harness from a personally used tool into a product through discovery research. The problem focus is context continuity when returning to work, switching tasks or sessions, and handing off work between humans, humans and AI, or AI agents.

Teammates is the chosen relationship framing for explaining the HAI collaboration layer. Broad exploration of alternative relationship models is not a research priority.

## Problem and design intent

Humans and AI need the relevant intentions, decisions, and unfinished work to continue across transitions. Investigate where that continuity breaks and what reconstruction or coordination effort it creates. Support both human orientation and AI continuation through the work itself, with minimal separate context-management burden.

## Discovery audience

- Nontechnical or less technical builders, including small business owners actively building with AI.
- Experienced builders who collaborate with other people or operate multiple agents.

The audience should expose practices beyond the creator's own profile and cover different kinds of transitions. Exact recruitment criteria remain open.

## Existing foundation

The current product provides durable context, task contracts, roles, and handoff mechanisms. These are an initial implementation, not proof of the eventual product solution. Research may simplify, replace, or remove mechanisms that do not improve continuity enough to justify their effort.

## Next step and unresolved scope

Define discovery research that helps choose the product direction. The research method, participant count, schedule, first prototype scenario, and final interface remain undecided. The suggested six interviews, two-week schedule, and fresh-session prototype are proposals, not commitments.

Confirmed choices are canonical in `decisions.md`.
